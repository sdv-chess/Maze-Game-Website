🧭 How to play Deep Delve

Goal

Guide your explorer from the entrance in the top-left corner to the amber 🔥 torch in the bottom-right corner.

Controls

Tap the on-screen arrows, or use WASD / the arrow keys, to move one cell at a time. You can't retrace your steps — trying to move back onto the cell you just came from is blocked with a message, so plan your route forward.

Hints & Unwind

Tap "👁️ Peek" to briefly light up the way forward from where you're standing. Tap "🔙 Unwind" (or press Backspace) to step back one cell — the one deliberate exception to the no-retracing rule. Both are limited: each maze grants a set number of uses, scaled to its size, so bigger mazes give you a bigger budget. Custom mazes let you set your own Hints and Unwinds counts when you generate them. Using either still counts toward your move total, and stepping into a dead end still permanently rules out a "clean" run even if you unwind back out of it.

Dead ends

Since you can't backtrack (beyond what Unwind allows), walking into a true dead end past your Unwind budget ends your run for good. Use "New maze" for a fresh layout, or "Restart" to try the same one again. If you're mid-run, "New maze" asks you to confirm first, since it discards that maze unless you already grabbed a save code for it.

Sizes & custom mazes

Pick Small, Medium or Large in the size panel, or choose Custom and set your own width and height (5–300 cells per side). However big the maze, each cell is always drawn at a fixed, legible size — if the maze is bigger than the window, the maze view scrolls instead of shrinking cells down to invisible specks. A small hint appears under the maze whenever that's the case.

Level mode

Choose "Level" in the size panel to play through an endless staircase of mazes that gets bigger and tougher every time. Level 1 starts small and square; from there, width and height grow at different rates level to level, so you'll get wide levels, tall levels, and everything in between rather than a plain square every time. By the higher levels the mazes are comfortably past 100 cells on a side.

Use "◀ Prev level" / "Next level ▶" to step through levels, or type a number into "Jump to level #" and tap "Go" to skip straight to it, then tap "Start Level" to generate it. After solving a level maze, the win screen offers a "Next level ▶" button to immediately move on to the next, harder maze. As with any large maze, the view scrolls to follow your character rather than shrinking cells away.

Save codes

Save codes are for pausing and resuming a single maze. Under "Save your run", tap "Get save code" to turn your current maze — walls, position and path — into a text code, then "Copy code" to copy it somewhere safe. To pick that maze back up (even in a different browser tab or later session), paste the code into the "Continue a run" box and tap "Load code" — you'll land back exactly where you left off, path still lit.

Accounts & history

Create an account to track your solved mazes and badges — it's saved to a file on your own device, never a server. Your solve history is grouped by maze category — Small, Medium, Large, Custom and Level Mode are all kept separate, so your custom-size solves and your Level mode progress never get mixed together.

Saving & reloading your account — quick steps

Your account, badges and history live in one save file, kept separate from single-maze save codes above. This is the part people miss most, so here's the short version:

1. In the Account card, type a username and password and tap Create account.

2. Right above that, tap Choose save folder if your browser offers it — this is the "set and forget" option: every solve auto-saves to that folder from then on, and the game reconnects to it automatically next time you open this page.

3. Don't see "Choose save folder," or want an extra backup anyway? Tap Download save file any time — that gives you a file named after your username (e.g. yourname.json). Keep it somewhere you'll find it (or re-download it after playing more).

4. Coming back later, on this device or a new one: if you'd linked a folder, it reconnects on its own — just sign in. Otherwise, tap Load save file, pick your downloaded yourname.json, then sign in to your account to pick up right where you left off.

Nothing here touches a server — everything lives in that one file, on your device, under your control. If you skip all four steps, your account still works for the current tab, but closing the tab (or clearing site data) without a linked folder or a downloaded file means that progress is gone for good.

Replay & practice

From your history, tap "▶ Replay & play" on any solved maze to step or play back your recorded run. Then click anywhere inside the maze to drop your character at that spot and move from there yourself — your practice path lights up red, separate from the recorded run. Backtracking rules don't apply here, so feel free to explore freely.

Badges

Earn badges for milestones, speed runs, flawless solves, Level mode progress and more. Tap any badge in the Badges panel to see its full name, rank and requirements, whether it's unlocked or not. The rarest one, "Deep Completionist," unlocks only once you've solved every single level from 1 through 100 — none skipped.

Themes

Pick a look for the cave from the Theme panel — every color in the maze and the interface switches with it. Your pick is remembered on this device, whether or not you're signed in.

Five themes are free from the start, plus a sixth, always-free Clear Sight theme built for colorblind accessibility — it swaps every red/green-dependent color pairing for a blue/orange/yellow palette with strong contrast, so walls, trail, and the finish marker stay distinguishable regardless of color vision. The rest are locked (🔒) behind Level mode, and they unlock strictly in order: a locked theme only opens up once you've solved every single level from 1 up to its unlock number, with none skipped. Jumping straight to a high level and solving it does not unlock anything by itself — you have to clear each level along the way first. Tap a locked theme to see exactly which level you still need.

Flair

Every badge you unlock also becomes a flair. Open the Flair panel and tap one to equip it beside your username in the Account card — tap it again (or "None") to remove it. Your equipped flair is saved with your account.

Sound

A soft tick plays with each step, a chime when you reach the torch, and a low tone if you hit a dead end. Tap "🔊 Sound: On" in the Actions card to mute or unmute — your choice is remembered on this device.
